# Indian City Hazard Authority Matrix

Research snapshot: 28 August 2026

This is a prototype routing guide, not a legal determination of ownership. In
Indian cities, the authority responsible for a hazard often depends on the exact
road, ward, asset and project. A city name plus hazard type is enough to suggest
a first router, but not enough to claim certainty.

## Routing order

Use this order before selecting an authority:

1. Identify the coordinates, municipal ward and road/asset owner.
2. Check whether the location is inside a municipal, development-authority,
   cantonment, port, airport, metro or railway estate.
3. Determine whether the road is municipal, state PWD/highways, NHAI, a
   development-authority road, or privately maintained.
4. For a utility cut, open chamber or exposed cable, route to the utility that
   owns the asset even when the surrounding road belongs to another authority.
5. If ownership remains uncertain, send to the municipal grievance system as
   the coordinating authority and visibly mark the routing confidence as low.

## Common emergency routing

| Situation | Immediate route | Civic follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Live wire, sparking transformer, electrical fire | Electricity emergency/1912; 112 or Fire 101 if there is immediate danger | Local distribution company |
| Gas leak, fire, structural collapse | 112 and Fire 101; utility emergency line where known | Fire service plus municipal building department |
| Person bitten by a suspected rabid animal | Hospital/anti-rabies clinic immediately; 112 if active danger | Municipal veterinary/public-health or animal-birth-control unit |
| Obstruction or failure at a railway crossing | 112/railway staff where immediate danger exists; RailMadad/139 | Relevant zonal railway plus approach-road owner |
| Hazard on a National Highway | NHAI 1033 | NHAI project implementation unit/concessionaire |

## Bengaluru

Governance note: Bengaluru is transitioning from the legacy BBMP structure to
the Greater Bengaluru Authority and city-corporation structure. Store both the
current corporation/zone and the legacy BBMP ward identifier while datasets and
portals catch up.

| Hazard | Likely first router | Important exceptions / co-owners |
|---|---|---|
| Potholes, damaged municipal roads | Relevant Bengaluru city corporation/GBA engineering or roads division | Karnataka PWD for state roads; NHAI for national highways; BDA for retained BDA layouts/roads; utility that made an un-restored cut |
| Footpaths, missing covers, encroachment | City corporation zonal engineering; estate/encroachment wing for obstruction | BDA, PWD, NHAI, Metro or private layout owner where applicable |
| Unsafe junction, road geometry, markings, signals | Road owner plus Bengaluru Traffic Police | BMLTA/DULT for network-level planning; NHAI/PWD/BDA for their corridors |
| Exposed wire, pole or transformer | BESCOM | City corporation for municipal street-light fixtures; KPTCL for transmission assets |
| Streetlight failure | City corporation/GBA street-light or electrical division | BESCOM where supply, service line or distribution equipment failed |
| Water leak, burst main, sewer/manhole | BWSSB | City corporation for storm-water drains and roadside surface drains |
| Open storm drain, flooding, rajakaluve | City corporation/GBA storm-water-drain division | BWSSB for sewage overflow; lake authority/GBA for lake outfalls; district disaster management during emergencies |
| Bridge, flyover, underpass | Named asset owner: city corporation, BDA, Karnataka PWD or NHAI | Railway over/under bridges may require South Western Railway and the road owner; Metro structures go to BMRCL |
| Railway crossing | South Western Railway | City corporation/PWD/NHAI/BDA for approach road; Traffic Police for traffic control |
| Garbage, black spot, dead animal, construction debris | City corporation solid-waste-management division | Bulk generator/private campus owner; Karnataka State Pollution Control Board for hazardous/industrial waste |
| Rabid/stray dogs | City corporation animal-husbandry/veterinary or contracted ABC unit | Health department/hospital for bites; police/112 for an active attack |
| Stray cattle | City corporation cattle/animal-husbandry enforcement | Traffic Police when immediately obstructing traffic |
| Dangerous building, falling facade, unsafe excavation | City corporation building-control/town-planning and zonal engineering | BDA planning area, BMRCL site, railway land or private construction regulator as applicable |
| Dangerous tree/branch | City corporation forest/horticulture division | Karnataka Forest Department for reserved forest; BESCOM when touching power lines |

Official starting points: [GBA/BBMP](https://bbmp.gov.in/),
[BESCOM](https://bescom.karnataka.gov.in/),
[BWSSB](https://bwssb.karnataka.gov.in/),

## Delhi

Delhi needs an ownership check before almost every road complaint. The same
neighbourhood may contain MCD, PWD Delhi, NDMC, DDA, NHAI, Delhi Cantonment and
utility-owned assets.

| Hazard | Likely first router | Important exceptions / co-owners |
|---|---|---|
| Potholes and road surface | MCD engineering for municipal/colony roads; PWD Delhi for PWD arterial roads | NDMC, DDA, NHAI or Delhi Cantonment according to ownership; DJB/utility for failed road restoration |
| Footpaths, encroachment, missing slabs | Owning road agency, usually MCD/PWD/NDMC | DDA land and markets; Traffic Police for an immediate obstruction |
| Road design, junctions, signals, markings | Owning road agency plus Delhi Traffic Police | UTTI/DDA for transport-infrastructure approvals; NHAI on national highways |
| Exposed electric wire/transformer | Area DISCOM: BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna or Tata Power-DDL | NDMC electricity in NDMC supply area; DTL for transmission assets |
| Streetlight failure | Owning civic/road agency or its lighting contractor | DISCOM when electricity supply or distribution equipment is the cause |
| Water leak, sewer overflow, open DJB chamber | Delhi Jal Board | MCD/PWD/NDMC for storm drains and road restoration |
| Open drain, waterlogging | Owning road/civic agency: MCD, PWD or NDMC | DJB for sewage; Irrigation & Flood Control for major drains; DDA for its land |
| Bridge, flyover, underpass | PWD Delhi or named owner | MCD, DDA, NHAI, DMRC and Northern Railway can each own particular structures |
| Railway crossing | Northern Railway | Road owner for approaches; Delhi Traffic Police for traffic management |
| Garbage, dumping, dead animal, C&D waste | MCD sanitation in MCD area; NDMC in NDMC area | Delhi Cantonment; DPCC for hazardous/industrial pollution; designated C&D operator/site |
| Rabid/stray dogs | MCD/NDMC veterinary services and authorised ABC operator | Hospital for bite treatment; 112 for immediate attack |
| Stray cattle | MCD/NDMC veterinary/cattle-catching service | Traffic Police for immediate road danger |
| Dangerous building/construction | MCD building department; NDMC/DDA in their jurisdictions | Delhi Fire Service for fire/life-safety emergency; district administration for collapse response |
| Dangerous tree | MCD/NDMC/PWD horticulture according to land owner | Delhi Forest Department for protected trees/forest land; DISCOM if touching wires |

Official starting points: [MCD](https://mcdonline.nic.in/),
[PWD Delhi](https://pwd.delhi.gov.in/),
[Delhi Jal Board](https://delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in/),
[NDMC](https://www.ndmc.gov.in/), [DDA](https://dda.gov.in/),
[BSES](https://www.bsesdelhi.com/),
[Tata Power-DDL](https://www.tatapower-ddl.com/),

## Mumbai

Within Greater Mumbai, BMC/MCGM is the normal coordinator, but MMRDA, MSRDC,
Maharashtra PWD, NHAI, port authorities and Railways own important corridors.

| Hazard | Likely first router | Important exceptions / co-owners |
|---|---|---|
| Potholes and damaged roads | BMC Roads and Traffic Department/ward office | MMRDA, MSRDC, PWD, NHAI, port or utility-cut owner |
| Footpaths, missing slabs, encroachment | BMC ward roads/maintenance and encroachment functions | MMRDA/MSRDC/PWD on their corridors; Traffic Police for immediate obstruction |
| Junction design, signals, markings | Road owner plus Mumbai Traffic Police | BMC traffic engineering; MMRDA/MSRDC/NHAI for controlled corridors |
| Exposed electric wire/transformer | Supply licensee by area: BEST, Adani Electricity, Tata Power or MSEDCL | Maharashtra transmission utility for high-voltage transmission |
| Streetlight failure | BMC/BEST or corridor owner, depending on location | MMRDA/MSRDC/NHAI for their roads |
| Water leak, sewer/manhole | BMC Hydraulic Engineer/Water Supply or Sewerage Operations | Storm-water drain issues go to BMC SWD |
| Open drain, flooding | BMC Storm Water Drains Department and ward | Sewerage Operations for sewage; MMRDA or other corridor owner where applicable |
| Bridge, flyover, subway | BMC Bridges Department for BMC structures | MMRDA, MSRDC, PWD, NHAI or Railways for named assets |
| Railway crossing | Western Railway or Central Railway by line | Approach-road owner and Mumbai Traffic Police |
| Garbage, dumping, dead animal, debris | BMC Solid Waste Management/ward | MPCB for hazardous/industrial waste; private/bulk generator duties |
| Rabid/stray dogs | BMC Veterinary Health Department/ABC programme | Municipal hospital for bites; 112 for active attack |
| Stray cattle | BMC veterinary/cattle-pound enforcement | Traffic Police for immediate obstruction |
| Dangerous building/construction | BMC Building Proposals/ward building and factory functions | MHADA, SRA, MMRDA, port or railway land according to jurisdiction |
| Dangerous tree | BMC Gardens/Tree Authority and ward | Utility for branches on live wires; private property owner subject to tree permission |

## National specialist routes

These routes apply across all seven cities:

| Asset/problem | Authority |
|---|---|
| National Highway carriageway, median, service road, highway structure | NHAI/project concessionaire; 1033 for highway incidents |
| Railway track, gate, signalling, railway bridge/subway within railway boundary | Relevant zonal railway; RailMadad/139 |
| Metro property | The city's metro rail corporation |
| Airport estate | Airport operator/AAI |
| Cantonment land | Cantonment Board/Defence Estates |
| Port estate | Port authority |
| Industrial pollution or hazardous waste | State Pollution Control Board; municipal fire service for an active emergency |
| Fire-safety violation | State/city Fire & Emergency Services; building sanctioning authority |
| Animal birth control and anti-rabies capture programme | Urban local body's veterinary/public-health function under the Animal Birth Control Rules |

National starting points: [NHAI](https://nhai.gov.in/),
[RailMadad](https://railmadad.indianrailways.gov.in/),
[Indian Railways](https://indianrailways.gov.in/), and
[Animal Welfare Board of India](https://awbi.gov.in/).

## Product implementation guidance

Do not encode this as a flat `city + hazard -> authority` map. Store:

- `jurisdiction_polygon`
- `asset_type`
- `asset_owner`
- `maintaining_agency`
- `complaint_channel`
- `emergency_channel`
- `source_url`
- `source_last_checked_at`
- `effective_from` and `effective_to`
- `routing_confidence`
- `fallback_authority`

The product should return:

1. `primary_authority`
2. `co_responsible_authorities`
3. `why_this_route`
4. `ownership_confidence`
5. `official_source`
6. `emergency_instruction`, when applicable

OpenAI can classify the hazard and extract landmarks. Authority selection should
be deterministic from verified geography and ownership data. If the system only
knows the city, say "suggested first router"; never imply exact jurisdiction.
